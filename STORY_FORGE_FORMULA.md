# 🎬 STORY FORGE — THE FORMULA

A video gets built in steps. Each step has ONE model that does the job.
You just describe what you want — Story Forge picks the right model.

| # | Step | What it does | Model |
|---|------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | **Picture** | Draws the still image | **Flux** |
| 2 | **Motion** | Makes the picture move | **Wan 2.2** (best) · **LTX** (fast b-roll) |
| 3 | **Talking person** | Realistic person speaking, WITH sound | **LTX-2** (video + audio) |
| 4 | **Voice** | Speaks the narration | **Piper (Ashley)** · **Chatterbox** |
| 5 | **Music** | Makes the soundtrack | **Song Forge** (ACE-Step) |

---

## Auto-pick: just describe it, Story Forge chooses the engine
- "a woman **talks to camera**" → **LTX-2** (makes the talking + the sound)
- "**wind** over a **wide** valley" → **LTX** (fast b-roll)
- "a man **walks** and **lifts** a box" → **Wan** (best action)

## The one idea to remember
There is **no single "make a video" button.** You line up one specialist
per job and stitch their work together. That's the whole thing.

## Not in the line (on purpose)
- **Hunyuan3D** — makes 3D objects, not video (CAD only).
