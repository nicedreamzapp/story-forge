# THE LAST SIGNAL — trailer script v3 (LOCKED 2026-10-31 2:52 AM, Matt: "Lock v3")
~50s, ~40 cuts, modern blockbuster pacing. Nothing static. A radio beep runs underneath and accelerates like a heartbeat.

## 0:00-0:08 HOOK (1-2s shots)
1. Beep. The ship rips through an asteroid field straight at camera.
   Sentiment: instant adrenaline.
2. Cockpit: red alarms flash, a hand slams a lever. SHIP COMPUTER (V.O.): "Uncharted."
   Sentiment: danger.
3. The ship dives into a wall of orange lightning.
   Sentiment: point of no return.

## 0:08-0:12 ARRIVAL (4s, the only held shot)
4. A COLOSSAL city-sized ship bursts out of the clouds; camera whips under its hull; its shadow swallows the land.
   SOUND: huge sub-bass rumble in 5.1 surround, front-to-rear sweep, LFE heavy (Matt 2:13a: "massive installation").
   Sentiment: overwhelming awe, the floor shakes.

## 0:12-0:22 REVEAL (1s cuts)
5. Landing claws slam into black glass sand; a shockwave of shattering glass rushes at camera.
6. Camera rockets through fog; a thousand carved monoliths explode into view to the horizon.
7. CLOSE: the Captain's hand on a symbol; it glows under her fingers. It is her own handwriting.
   Sentiment: the chill.
8. Every monolith lights up at once, a wave of light circling the planet.
   Sentiment: wonder turning to fear.

## 0:22-0:25
9. THE PLANET (V.O., deep, from everywhere): "Welcome back, Captain." The beep stops dead.
   Sentiment: the gut-punch.

## 0:25-0:42 RAMP (half-second cuts, bass hit on each)
10. A second sun tears the sky open.
11. The Crewmate is yanked off his feet into the fog.
12. The engines explode in blue fire.
13. A mountain-sized shape rises behind the monoliths.
14. Glass cracks open under the Captain's boots as she sprints.
15. The ship lifts off; something huge grabs it.
16. Strobe flashes, accelerating: light, eye, claw, fire, monolith, scream.
    Sentiment: panic, overload.

## 0:42-0:45
17. BLACK. One second of silence. CAPTAIN (whisper): "I've never been here."
    Sentiment: the question that stays.

## 0:45
18. TITLE SLAM: THE LAST SIGNAL. Final beep, final bass hit.
    Sentiment: wanting more.
