# THE LAST SIGNAL — premise (trailer, ~60s)

Matt's idea, his words: "Space, new planets, aliens, spacecraft, wonder" → "New sci-fi story" → "Trailer" → "impactful".

**Controlling idea:** The unknown is most terrifying when it already knows you.
**Who wants what:** A cargo-ship captain (woman, 40s) follows an uncharted signal to find out what it is.
**What stops her:** The planet remembers her — her own handwriting is carved on alien monoliths — and it wakes up.
**Ending (trailer):** No answer. She whispers "I've never been here." Title slam. The question is the hook.
