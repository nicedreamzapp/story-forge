# Story test — THE LAST SIGNAL (written 2026-10-31 ~3:30 AM, from beats.json alone)

## What a first-time viewer would say, from the shot list alone
A huge spaceship tears through an asteroid field while its woman captain fights the controls under red alarms, then dives into an orange lightning storm. It turns out to be colossal, the size of a city, and it lands hard on a black glass plain covered with endless carved monoliths. The captain and a crewmate walk among them; when she touches a carved symbol it glows under her hand and her face shows she recognizes it. Every monolith on the planet lights up in a wave, and she stands frozen while the planet itself speaks to her. Then everything attacks at once: a second sun rips the sky open, the crewmate is dragged off into the fog, the ship's engines explode, a mountain-sized living thing rises behind the monoliths, and she runs as the glass cracks under her boots while something enormous grabs the ship as it tries to escape. Black. She whispers that she has never been here.

## The premise, for comparison
The unknown is most terrifying when it already knows you. A cargo-ship captain follows an uncharted signal; the planet remembers her (her own handwriting is carved on its monoliths) and it wakes up. No answer; she whispers "I've never been here." The question is the hook.

They match: danger, arrival, recognition, the planet waking, the escape failing, the unanswered question.
