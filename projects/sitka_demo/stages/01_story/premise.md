# Premise (sitka_demo, a test fixture cut from the approved Sitka promo of 2026-10-22)

## Who it is for
Makers and people who stand or squat all day and want a seat they wear.

## The problem
Exoskeletons fight the body with motors and metal.

## What the product changes
Sitka adds what muscles do, with springs, struts and cables, and it is fully open.

## The one thing to remember
Sit anywhere, and come build one with us.

| Line | Text | Sentiment |

| 1 | This is Sitka. Strong, light... | quiet awe, "that's beautiful" |
| 2 | Sit down anywhere... | relief, then "oh, that's clever" |
| 3 | Sitka is completely open... | invitation, belonging |
