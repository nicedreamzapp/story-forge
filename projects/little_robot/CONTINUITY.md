## Why the sacrifice WORKS mechanically (fastening logic)
The bike's "heart" = drivetrain: chainring/sprocket, chain links, bearings,
seat spring. Sprocket's body maps onto it part-for-part — that's the point of
his name:
- brass wagon-wheel leg → the bike's CHAINRING (brass spoked gear, visibly the
  same wheel in scene 4-5 bike shots)
- blue spring-piston leg → the bike's seat spring (blue, visible under saddle)
- copper clip-latches from his chest → chain master links
- bright blue LEFT eye lens → clipped into the work lamp so he can finish the
  job one-handed in the dark (lamp glows BLUE in st_eye_lamp)
Every given part appears ON the bike afterward. Nothing vanishes.
