# HANK & DOUG'S WILD RESCUE — theme song
Written 2026-11-17. Lyrics only; nothing rendered. Normal capitalization, no humans, canon words only.

## What it has to do
Carry the montage from 0:11 to 0:48 of the trailer (verse + chorus, then a soft instrumental for the flashback,
then the chorus again landing its last line on the title card). Be singable by a six-year-old after one listen:
short lines, a shouted call-and-response, sound words (SPLOOSH), and a list a kid can count on fingers (horse,
zebra, camel, cow). Hank's catchphrase "Rolling" is the song's tag so the button gag pays it off.

## Lyrics

### Verse 1 (8 bars, ~16 s at 120 BPM)
When a goat's stuck high and a turtle's stuck low,
And a kitten is stuck in a drain,
Some little critter is always gonna know,
And whistle and holler our names!

### Pickup (2 bars)
Who's that coming? (Who's that coming?)
Rollin', rollin', here we go!

### Chorus (8 bars, ~16 s)
Hank and Doug! (Hank and Doug!)
A horse and a zebra, a camel, a cow!
Hank and Doug! (Hank and Doug!)
Giraffes are the ladders, we're coming right now!
Elephants pump and the water goes SPLOOSH!
Nobody's too big and nobody's too small,
Somebody always hears the call...
It's Hank and Doug's Wild Rescue!

### Verse 2 (optional, full-length version for episodes, not used in the trailer)
Doug had a nose that could follow a trail,
Till he met a big bear in the dark,
He picked a new side and he wagged his old tail,
And the two of 'em made a fresh start!

### Tag / outro (2 bars)
Rollin'! (Rollin'!) Here we go!
