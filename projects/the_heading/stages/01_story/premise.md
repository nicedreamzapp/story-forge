# The Heading — premise (story spine by Fable, 11/07 1:42 AM)

**Avoiding (originality gate):**
1. Amnesiac wakes from cryosleep, finds the bridge, the logs say he is captain.
2. "It was a simulation / he's an AI / he's dying in a hospital bed."
3. Generation ship where everyone forgot they are aboard and he finds the hull.

**Angle:** nobody forgot and nobody is hiding it. The town has always known the house on the bluff keeps the heading and arranged its life around his routines, leaving him alone because a sitter who is pushed steers badly. He read their deference as being ignored. The new thing is that he says yes, before the tilt passes what habit can correct.

**Logline:** a radio reporter films the last house on the Humboldt coast with no signal and finds her footage of its owner keeps coming back painted, clearing only as he understands that three hundred people are living on his mornings, and the mornings are running out.

**Matt's notes folded in:** he is the captain and everyone is counting on him; urgency climbs through the whole film.

**Him:** Wes Harlan, 57, fixes vaporizer heaters in a one-room shop in Trinidad, lives alone on the bluff in the no-signal house. Same chair at dawn, a 4:10 AM generator reset every ninth night, a notebook of fog and tide. Wants to sell and move somewhere his phone works. Believes nothing he does carries past the counter.

**Device:** Nia, community-radio reporter, shoots a piece on the dead-zone house. Her camera records what Wes understands as real and what he doesn't yet as paint. Only her voice and his.

**The clock:** the horizon tilt through his window, 2° at open, climbing all film; the reset interval shrinks with it (ninth night → nightly → hourly).

**Clue trail:** 0:30 tilted horizon, level reads true · 3:00 dead circle on coverage maps, "that's the bluff house" · 6:00 older, larger grooves under his on the chair arms · 9:00 first hybrid: room real, window paint · 12:00 clinic, school bus, fleet all key off his 4:10 · 15:00 notebook column grows since the last sitter died · 18:00 GPS-stacked drives form an arc, bending harder · 21:00 drives to sign the sale, road ahead is paint, town behind the only real thing, turns back · 24:00 six sitters' notebooks left at his door, clinic dark, reset hourly.

**Reveal (~26:00):** no speech. He sits; the room goes photoreal from the chair outward, window last. Writes "heading" atop the empty column, does the reset awake and knowing, the horizon comes level for the first time. Cancels the sale. The clinic's lights come back across the cove.

**Last image:** the window, real and level, gray coast sliding left at the speed of a tide, his hand on the armrest.
