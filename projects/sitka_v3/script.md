# Sitka promo v3 (~90 s, Matt's voice). Every number matches run-sit-exo PARTS_LIST.md / KINETICS.md / TENDON_CONCEPT.md.

Each numbered line is one voice clip. [brackets] = the picture under it.

1. [knee macro, clutch macro, then the hero reveal on "Sitka works with it"] Most exoskeletons fight your body with motors and metal. Sitka works with it.
2. [title slam on "Sitka", golden-hour bluff, strut / run / leaf spring on strong, light, bends] This is Sitka. Named after the tall Sitka spruce trees on the bluffs of Trinidad, California. Strong, light, and it bends without breaking.
3. [ghosted walk (bones), springs, clutch, tendons glow on "tendons"] Your bones already carry the weight. Sitka adds what your muscles do, with springs, clutches, and cables that work like tendons.
4. [sit down outdoors, strut close-up on "pole", knee spring glow on "spring"] Sit down anywhere, and a light pole under each hip takes most of your weight. A carbon spring at each knee catches you, and gives that energy back when you stand.
5. [lean back, the two Crazy Creek straps] Lean back, and two simple straps hold you, like a camp chair you wear.
6. [stoop lift of a box, back tendon glows, lift diagram with 15 and 12 on their words] Bend to lift, and a tendon along your back stores energy. A fifteen kilo box feels about twelve kilos lighter, with no motor at all.
7. [smooth walk then run on the bluff, bands glow, band diagram] Walking and running, elastic bands link your hips, knees, and ankles, the way your own hamstrings and calves do.
8. [clutch flicks on at stance, the back motor, real stair climb on "stairs", hills] Want more? Smart clutches switch the springs on at exactly the right moment. And one small motor on your back helps only where you need it, on stairs, hills, and heavy lifts.
9. [modules dock into the hub; kit card "about 14 lb", "~$2,000 a set, small batches" on their words] It's fully modular. The seat and back kit weighs about fourteen pounds, and runs around two thousand dollars a set when it's built in small batches. Then add hips, ankles, shoulders, and power, all docking into one hub.
10. [a shell printing layer by layer, then the kit split into printed / metal / bought, parts list scroll] Most of the frame is 3D printed on a desktop printer, and every part is listed with its price, on GitHub.
11. [every part exploded, finale on the bluff, end card slam AFTER "us"] Sitka is completely open. The design, the math, every part. Come build one with us.
