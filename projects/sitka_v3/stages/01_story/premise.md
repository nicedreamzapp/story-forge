# Premise — Sitka promo v3 (2026-10-22)

## Who it is for
Makers, rehab and wearables people, and anyone who stands or lifts all day: people who would build or
fund an open exoskeleton if they could see how it works and what it really costs.

## The problem
Most exoskeletons fight the body with motors and metal: heavy, expensive, closed. The v1 promo of this
design was pulled because its numbers were wrong ("$1,300, under 10 pounds").

## What the product changes
Sitka works with the body: bones carry the load, springs, clutches and cable tendons do what muscles do,
one small motor only where it is needed. It is modular (start with the seat + back kit) and fully open.

## The one thing to remember
An open, buildable exoskeleton, with honest counted numbers: the seat + back kit is about 14 lb and about
$2,000 a set in small batches (PARTS_LIST.md: 6.31 kg; $1,925 a set in a batch of 10; $2,671 for
prototype one). Design targets, not yet measured on a prototype.
