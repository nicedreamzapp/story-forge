## Who it is for
Makers, hikers and anyone whose legs get tired: people who would build or wear an open-source
exoskeleton, and the curious who just want to see how it works.

## The problem
Your legs get tired out on the trail, and there is never a seat where you need one.

## What the product changes
Sitka is an exoskeleton that helps every step (a motor at each knee) and turns into a chair
anywhere: a carbon rear leg swings down behind each thigh, and a steel ratchet locks the knee.

## The one thing to remember
A stool you wear: it helps you walk and run, then lets you sit anywhere, and it is completely open.
