# Sitka promo, narration v1 (~90 s, Matt's voice)

Each line is one voice clip. [brackets] = the picture under it.

1. [low hero orbit, running] What if your legs could help you run... and then turn into a chair, anywhere?
2. [title slam: SITKA, then spruce on the Trinidad bluffs] This is Sitka. Named for the Sitka spruce on the bluffs of Trinidad, California. Strong, light, and it bends without breaking.
3. [on-body walk, hike, run; callout on the knee motor] Out on the trail, a motor at each knee pushes as your leg straightens. Walking, hiking, running, it's with you on every step.
4. [slowing to a stop, seat offer buzz, HUD] Stop moving, and it notices. One tap, and you sit.
5. [rear leg deploy, side view] Here's the science. A carbon leg swings down behind each thigh and plants on the ground.
6. [chair orbit with CoM dot and support triangle] Now each side stands on three points. Your heel, your toes, and that rear foot. Your weight lands inside that triangle, with almost a foot of room behind you.
7. [sit diagram, red vs green] No counterweights. No tricks. Just a stool you wear.
8. [knee close-up, ratchet] A steel ratchet with two pawls locks each knee. It holds you even with the power off. And when you stand up, it simply lets go.
9. [explode + assembly, part callouts] Ninety-three pieces. Two Cube Mars knee motors. Two battery packs on the belt. A Teensy brain, motion sensors on each leg, and that carbon rear leg.
10. [weight card] All in, about twenty pounds.
11. [assembly, build card] A first prototype takes about six to ten weeks. Off-the-shelf motors, machined aluminum, and open-source control code from Northern Arizona University.
12. [production card] At a hundred units, the frames and cuffs get molded, and every unit gets a load test before it ships.
13. [end card: SITKA, walk, run, sit anywhere + GitHub link] Sitka is completely open. The design, the math, every part. Come build one with us.
