sentence's first word. Every fact here is verified (stages/01_story/premise.md lists the sources).

2. [spec stack builds: two H3 models blended on an S-curve, 50 blocks, 33B] On paper it's a dream. Two MiniMax H3 models, blended across fifty blocks, thirty three billion parameters in all.
3. [two LoRA chips snap in, 4-8 steps; size bar shrinks 61 GB to 19 GB] Plus two distillation adapters folded in, for renders in four to eight steps. And it's squeezed from over sixty one gigs down to about nineteen.
4. [our consensus ranking, H3 at number one] And H3 ranked number one in our own local video research.
5. [STEP 1: LICENSE card; the HF tag crossed out, the LICENSE file opened] But every new model starts at step one, which is the license. The actual license file, not the tag on the page.
6. [base_model chain: the hybrid back to MiniMax H3] Every merge inherits its base model's license. So we followed it all the way back to MiniMax H3.
7. [the Excluded Territories clause, highlighted line by line] And there it is. Excluded territories means the European Union, the United Kingdom, Korea, and the United States. Outputs included.
8. [California pin, red X; MLX port to Hugging Face crossed out] We're in California, so it's out. And putting an MLX port on Hugging Face would count as distributing it, so that's a no.
9. [fit check: int8 only, ComfyUI only, we run bf16] It also only ships in eight bit, and only runs in Comfy UI. We run everything at full precision.
10. [what we use now: Kandinsky 6 Pro card, stats tick in] So what do we use instead? We use Kandinsky 6 Pro. It has an MIT license, twenty nine billion parameters, and takes about twelve minutes per three second clip on the M5 Max.
11. [Matt's quote card; LTX-2.5 card joins side by side] On its very first clip, I said that's the best I've ever seen. And we use LTX for quick previews and lip sync.
12. [the 7-step checklist ticking on each word] Now every new model goes through the same seven steps. License, fit, compare, test, convert, verdict, and a review video like this one.
13. [a door left open: Apache 2.0 with a question mark] If the license on MiniMax H3 ever changes, and their team said maybe, no promises, we'll port it.
14. [the checklist settles, end card slam AFTER "build"] That's how we pick our tools. Read the license first, then build.
