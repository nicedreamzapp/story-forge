# Story test — HANK & DOUG'S WILD RESCUE (from the shot list alone, no dialogue, no narration)
2026-10-31 11:10 PM

## What a first-time viewer would say it is about (shots only)
At night in a forest a bloodhound is tracking a bear, with a lantern swinging behind him like someone is
following. The bear is hiding, terrified, behind a log. The dog stops face to face with the bear, their eyes
meet, and instead of giving him away the dog bolts off down a different path with the lantern light chasing
him; the next thing we see is the lantern tumbling into a muddy ditch. So the hunting dog switched sides and
dumped his owner in a ditch to save the bear. Now, in daylight: a train's wheels are screaming on the rails,
and the alarm passes from a jackrabbit to a hawk to a prairie dog. A big red wooden fire-wagon on giant
wooden wheels bursts out of a barn, pulled by horses, camels and cows, giraffes riding on it, elephants with
water barrels running alongside, and that same bloodhound up front beside a big brown bear holding the reins.
A circus train is racing across the desert toward tracks that just end at a canyon. The bear and the dog look
at the gap. Then the ground starts moving: a river of ants wearing little metal plates pours toward the canyon,
links into a living bridge over the gap while giraffes peer down, an elephant trumpets, the train whistle
blasts, a monkey conductor covers his eyes, the dog holds his breath, and the train's wheels reach the edge.
Black. The title. A tiny ant salutes the dog and the dog salutes back.

## Against the premise
Premise: "the hunter who switched sides now leads the whole animal kingdom to the rescue."
- Switched sides: SHOWN without words (c01a hunt -> c01b frightened bear -> c01c eye contact = the choice ->
  c01d dog leads the lantern away -> c01e lantern in the ditch). The line "I picked a side" confirms, it does
  not carry it.
- Leads the whole animal kingdom: the relay (rabbit, hawk, prairie dog), the crew (team, giraffes, elephants),
  Hank and Doug up front, then the ants answering the call. SHOWN.
- No humans: the hunter exists only as a lantern from above frame and a lantern in a ditch. Holds.
- The ending cuts to black before the train hits the bridge (cliffhanger), then the button. Holds.

## Gaps found and fixed
1. v0 had the dog only "looking back" with no shot of the bear and dog seeing each other: the choice was a
   guess. Added c01c_eyes (R03, a composite two-shot) so the choice is a visible state.
2. v0 had the ditch with nothing tying it to the dog. c01d_away now shows the lantern beam following the dog
   down a side path, AWAY from the log, so the ditch reads as his doing.
3. The ants needed a "who" so the button lands: R22 introduces the ant hero (dented plate) in the reveal, and
   the same ant salutes in c10a.
4. Open (accepted): the bear in the origin is drawn as a younger Hank, which a viewer may or may not read as
   "this is how they met". It never contradicts the script; it only adds.
