## Scenes (screenplay)
**1. EXT/INT. COUNTRY ROAD / THE WAGON — DAY, RAIN.** Black; a music box plays the lullaby, whole and right. Sun through
an old kitchen window; the first rain on the glass. A wood-paneled wagon on a road under tall trees. In the back, LILY (7)
holds a music box to her chest and opens it; the dancer turns. CLAIRE (late 30s) drives, eyes in the mirror.
CLAIRE (O.S.): "Almost there, bug."  TOM (40s) sleeps against the passenger window. The wagon climbs the wet drive to the
farmhouse. RUTH (70s) waits in the doorway, holding the screen door. Lily runs through the rain into her arms.
RUTH (O.S.): "Look how big you got."  Claire, a box on her hip, looks up at the house she grew up in.
CLAIRE: "Hi, Mom."  Ruth touches her face, a beat too long, like checking.  RUTH (O.S.): "You cut your hair."
CLAIRE (O.S.): "Five years ago, Mom."  Claire's smile holds; her eyes don't.  Tom carries boxes past the wall of photos.
Lily stops under the photos, then runs up the stairs; the camera stays on the empty stairs a beat too long.

**2. INT. THE HOUSE — AFTERNOON TO DUSK.** Unpacking. Claire unwraps dishes; Ruth watches from the table. Tom gets an old lamp
to flicker on.  LILY (O.S., upstairs): "Mom! There's already a chair up here!"  In the attic room, among boxes, a small
blue child's chair in the corner.  CLAIRE (O.S.): "Where did this come from?"  Lily sits on it; her knees come up high.
LILY: "It's too small for me."  On the chair back, a name has been scratched off the wood.  CLAIRE (O.S., calling down):
"Mom? Whose chair is this?"  Ruth at the foot of the stairs looks up, and says nothing, and goes back to the kitchen.
By the front door Tom hangs coats: four hooks at adult height, one low hook at a child's height. He hangs Lily's yellow
raincoat on the low one.  TOM (O.S.): "Perfect height for you, Lil."  Lily puts the music box on her shelf. In her bedroom,
on the wallpaper, a clean rectangle where a second bed once stood; Claire, making Lily's bed, stops at it.
Dusk: Tom and Claire eat takeout on moving boxes.  TOM: "We did it."  Claire laughs, then looks over: Ruth sets out
place mats, counting under her breath.  RUTH (O.S.): "...three, four, five."  CLAIRE (O.S.): "Mom. There's four of us."
Ruth looks at the five mats as if she can't see the problem.

**3. INT. LIVING ROOM — NIGHT, RAIN.** Claire brings tea. Ruth stands at the rainy window with her back to the room.
CLAIRE (O.S.): "Mom? You okay?"  In the glass, Ruth's reflection; her eyes are somewhere else.
RUTH (O.S.): "Some things you forget... because you have to."  CLAIRE (O.S.): "Forget what?"  Ruth turns and looks at
her daughter like a stranger for one breath, then smiles.  RUTH (O.S.): "Is it time for bed?"  Claire is losing her mother.

**HOOK. INT. FRONT HALL — NIGHT.** The coat rack in the dark. Lily's yellow raincoat on the low hook, and beside it, a small
boy's blue raincoat that wasn't there. It is dripping. A drop hits the floorboards. Upstairs, the music box plays one note:
the wrong one. Black. Card: THE THINGS WE REMEMBER · PART ONE.
