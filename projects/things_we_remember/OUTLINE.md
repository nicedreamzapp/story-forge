# THE THINGS WE REMEMBER — feature outline v1 (2026-11-13)

Nice Dreamz Pictures presents, a Story Forge production. Supernatural horror, ~90 min. No narration.

**Controlling idea:** we survive grief by forgetting, and the forgetting is what feeds it.
**The secret (never fully explained):** for generations, the women of this family have given a child's memory to the Rememberer so they could keep living. It takes the grief and in return it is remembered. Ruth gave up a son. Claire gave up Daniel.

**Cast:** CLAIRE (late 30s, the mother) · TOM (her husband) · LILY (7) · RUTH (Claire's mother, 70s, memory failing) · DANIEL (5, the forgotten son) · THE REMEMBERER (a gentle, ordinary-looking man in old clothes; never shown clearly before the midpoint)

**The melody:** a lullaby on music box and piano. One note changes whenever the Rememberer is near. Only the last shot plays the missing note.

## ACT 1 — Warmth (0–25 min)
1. Opening image: sun through a kitchen window, rain later, a music box lullaby. Claire, Tom and Lily move into Claire's childhood home to look after Ruth. (Sentiment: nostalgia, safety.)
2. Unpacking. Small wrongs nobody comments on: a child's chair nobody remembers buying, a second hook at the coat rack at kid height. (Faint unease.)
3. Ruth, staring out the window: "Some things you forget because you have to." (The theme, stated.)
4. A woman drops a glass. Behind the doorway, a shape turns its head toward the sound before the glass hits. Nobody sees it. [TEASER]
5. **Inciting incident (~12 min): THE EXTRA PERSON.** A photo has a man behind Claire's parents. "Your uncle." "Mom, I don't have an uncle." "Not anymore." Silence. [TEASER]
6. Claire puts the photo away. That night the melody plays with one wrong note.
7. **Plot point 1 (~25 min): THE SECOND BED.** Footsteps upstairs. Two beds in Lily's room. "Don't wake her." The blanket is empty. Lily is awake: "Mommy, you weren't supposed to see him." Claire decides to find out who "him" is. [TRAILER]

## ACT 2A — Something is wrong (25–55 min)
8. Claire searches the house: an attic of boxes, a family tape recorder, a wiped page in a baby book.
9. **THE FAMILY DINNER (~35 min).** Five plates. "For Daniel." "Who's Daniel?" Everyone stops. Lily: "He's eating with us." The camera pushes in on the empty chair. The fork moves. [TEASER]
10. Next morning Tom has no memory of saying "Daniel." (Paranoia: is it Claire?)
11. Lily's drawings: a family of five, with one figure scribbled out.
12. A cassette of a birthday party with everyone laughing. Under the laughter a child whispers: "Why did you forget me?" The tape plays on as if nothing happened. [TRAILER]
13. B story: Ruth's lucid moments. She half-recognizes the boy's name, then loses it. The mother-daughter bond the Rememberer will use.
14. **Midpoint (~55 min): THE HOME VIDEO.** Lily at three in the garden, Tom filming. "Daddy, who's that man behind you?" ... "He says you've forgotten him again." A small boy's hand reaches into the frame. Claire hears his laugh and **remembers it.** A false victory: she is getting him back. Then the same little voice speaks from behind her, and Lily is asleep upstairs. [TRAILER]

## ACT 2B — Paranoia (55–75 min)
15. The more Claire remembers, the more the Rememberer shows up: in doorways, at the end of the table, in the oven door's reflection, outside the bedrooms while everyone sleeps. It never runs, never chases.
16. Tom thinks Claire is going the way of her mother. The marriage splits.
17. Ruth's confession, fully lucid: "I had a boy too. I gave him to it, so I could get out of bed." She can't say his name.
18. **All is lost (~75 min): HUNDREDS OF PHOTOS.** The same man in every one: Claire's childhood, her parents' wedding, her grandparents, photos older than the grandparents. Her family hasn't been haunted. It has been remembering him for generations. That night Ruth dies in her sleep. In the morning Tom looks at Lily's bed and asks, "Whose bed is that?" Now Lily is being forgotten.

## ACT 3 — Dread (75–90 min)
19. Dark night: Claire works out the trade. Remember Daniel and lose Lily, or forget Daniel forever and keep her. Two terrible choices.
20. **Climax:** she chooses Lily. She burns the photos and the tape, closes the music box, and says goodbye to a son she can barely picture. The Rememberer stands behind her, gentle: "I remember you before you were born." She forgets. Tom calls Lily by name again.
21. Resolution: months pass and the house is warm again. (A false peace.)
22. **Final scene: THE DRAWING.** "Who are you drawing?" "Someone who misses you." Claire beside a little boy. She recognizes him: her son. "See, Mommy? You remember him now." In the window's reflection, the Rememberer smiles. Cut to black. The lullaby plays the missing note. "Thank you for remembering." [TEASER end card]

## Rules (from the director)
The audience sees less than the characters know · the demon is scariest acting normal · sound before image · family love is the weapon · never fully explain.
