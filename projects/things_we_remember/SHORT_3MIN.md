## Story test (from the shots alone)
A family moves into grandma's old house. Claire finds a photo with a man in it her mother calls "your uncle", then
"not anymore". At dinner her husband sets a plate for "Daniel" and then can't say who Daniel is; the little girl says
he's eating with them, and the fork moves. That night there's a boy in a second bed who vanishes, the same man is in
every family photo for a hundred years, and he tells Claire he remembers her. Months later the girl draws Claire with
a little boy, and Claire recognizes her own son; the girl says "you remember him now", and the man in the window
smiles and thanks her. → Matches the premise: the remembering is what feeds it.
