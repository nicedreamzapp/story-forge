# Premise — THE THINGS WE REMEMBER (45-second teaser, first rung of the feature)
Matt's idea, his words: "Make an audience feel that something is terribly wrong without showing them anything obviously supernatural for as long as possible." Full director's vision and the 22-scene feature outline: ../../OUTLINE.md.
- Controlling idea: we survive grief by forgetting, and the forgetting is what feeds it.
- Who wants what: Claire wants to know who the stranger in her family's life is. What stops her: the family (and the Rememberer) want him forgotten, and remembering costs her Lily.
- Ending (feature): she forgets to save Lily, then remembers; the Rememberer smiles. Teaser ends on the title and "Why did you forget me?"
