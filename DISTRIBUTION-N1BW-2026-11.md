# Distribution option: N1BW (checked 2026-11-21)

N1BW (https://n1bw.com) is a platform for short serialized series, started by Roy Price, who founded Amazon Studios. It takes AI-made work, and the creator keeps ownership. If we make a series we like, this is a place to put it.

## Why it fits

Roy Price's 11/20 post predicts a vertical-series platform that moves past soap operas into comedy, sci-fi and action, mostly AI-made, with "vertical series as the new comic books" (characters that win on phones move up to TV). That describes Story Forge's output, Hank & Doug included.

## Format (from their Upload Rules)

- Episodes: 45 s minimum, 1 to 3 min recommended, 150 min max
- Shape: 9:16 or 2:3 vertical, or 16:9 horizontal
- Resolution: 480x854 minimum, 720x1280 recommended
- A season needs at least 5 episodes
- Trailers: 15 s to 3 min, up to 500 MB
- A person reviews every upload

## AI and content rules

- AI-made work is allowed. The only AI rule bans a real person's face or voice used without their permission (your own likeness is fine).
- Not allowed: sexual content, illegal content, material you don't have rights to, deepfakes, harassment.

## Money and rights (from the Participation Agreement)

- Free episodes earn nothing. Paid access is priced from $1.99 to $19.99.
- Creators get 70% of net revenue, or $0.10 per paid transaction, whichever is more. That 70% is split among the people who made the show; a solo creator keeps all of it.
- Payouts come about 45 days after each month once the balance passes $50. Up to 10% can be held for 90 days against refunds.
- The license is non-exclusive: the same series can go on YouTube, TikTok and elsewhere, and can be pulled from N1BW at any time.
- The creator owns the IP.

## Easiest first step

AutoPilot is a weekly pitch contest. Pitching is free and needs no account (online or 1-877-612-9880). Viewers vote, voting closes Thursday at midnight Eastern, and the winning pitch becomes a short promo that goes up Friday.
